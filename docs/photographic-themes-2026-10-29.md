# Photographic Fiji, Pacific and tropical themes

Room themes now use photographic island imagery rather than flat illustrations. The 94 theme choices retain their IDs, names, palettes, community credits and saved settings. A shared pool of natural ocean, reef, rainforest, flowers, music, community and school-themed photographs keeps the collection cohesive and avoids downloading a separate large image for every colour variant.

Four new photorealistic images were made with the built-in image generator: tropical reef, rainforest stream, coconut beach and hibiscus/frangipani. Existing Sota Kece photographic island and community artwork supplies the remaining subjects. The manifest records source files, generation prompts, hashes and delivered dimensions. These are atmospheric generated or existing artistic scenes, not documentary photographs of named schools, artists or places.

The photographic catalogue and delivery assets live under revised/assets/skins/photographic-20261029/. The native generator consumes this catalogue and applies the same photo covers to native room and chat surfaces. Original institutional labels, song references and older artwork remain preserved; flat vector covers are retained only as source history and are no longer the active theme art.

Room Studio uses a quieter gallery frame without the illustrated sun/wave header or cover zoom. All search, preview, apply, cancel, reset, sticky selection controls and account isolation remain. The page designer's six island palettes, fonts, buttons, layout and wallpaper controls remain unchanged. Photograph files load and cache only on demand; the existing public cache boundaries and small installation budget still apply.

This corrects the artwork presentation only. It does not change account data, circle metadata, database mappings, music behaviour or the separate portfolio typography work. The working preview remains held for owner review before publication.
